Problem 3. The function $T$ is defined by $T(h) = 68 - 3h$, where $T(h)$ is the temperature, in degrees Fahrenheit, of a greenhouse $h$ hours after noon. Which of the following is the best interpretation of the number 3 in this context?

B) The temperature of the greenhouse decreases by $3$ degrees Fahrenheit each hour after noon.

C) The temperature of the greenhouse increases by $3$ degrees Fahrenheit each hour after noon.

D) It takes $3$ hours for the temperature of the greenhouse to reach $68$ degrees Fahrenheit.

Problem 26. A landscaper mows lawns and trims hedges. Mowing one lawn takes $1.5$ hours and pays \$45, and trimming one hedge takes $2.5$ hours and pays \$70. The landscaper can work at most $40$ hours this week and wants to earn at least \$1,000. If $m$ is the number of lawns mowed and $h$ is the number of hedges trimmed, which system of inequalities represents this situation?

A) $1.5m + 2.5h \le 40$ and $45m + 70h \ge 1000$

B) $1.5m + 2.5h \ge 40$ and $45m + 70h \le 1000$

C) $1.5m + 2.5h \le 40$ and $45m + 70h \le 1000$

D) $45m + 70h \le 40$ and $1.5m + 2.5h \ge 1000$

Problem 27. In the $xy$-plane, the graph of $y = x^2 - 6x + c$, where $c$ is a constant, intersects the $x$-axis at the points $(p, 0)$ and $(q, 0)$, where $q - p = 4$. What is the value of $c$?

Answer Key
1. C — [Algebra: Linear equations in one variable] Distribute: $5x - 10 = 3x + 8$, so $2x = 18$ and $x = 9$.
2. C — [Geometry/Trig: Area and volume] $V = \pi r^2 h = \pi(3^2)(10) = 90\pi$.
3. B — [Algebra: Linear functions] The coefficient of $h$ is the rate of change: each hour, $T$ changes by $-3$ degrees.
4. 6 — [Algebra: Linear equations in two variables] Set $y = 0$: $4x = 24$, so $x = 6$.
5. A — [Advanced Math: Nonlinear equations in one variable] $x^2 = 25$, and $x > 0$ gives $x = 5$.
6. B — [Algebra: Linear functions] $f(5) = 15 - 4 = 11$ and $g(2) = 8$, so $f(5) - g(2) = 3$.
7. C — [Algebra: Systems of two linear equations] Substitute: $3x + (2x - 1) = 14$, so $x = 3$ and $y = 5$; $x + y = 8$.
8. $-5$ — [Advanced Math: Equivalent expressions] Expand: $2x^2 - 8x + 3x - 12 = 2x^2 - 5x - 12$, so $b = -5$.
9. C — [PSDA: Percentages] Sale price $80(0.75) = 60$; with tax, $60(1.08) = 64.80$. Choice B applies the tax before the discount to the wrong base; A forgets the tax.
10. C — [Algebra: Linear inequalities in one or two variables] Test $(2, 2)$: $2 \ge -4 + 5 = 1$ and $2 < 3$, both true. $(1, 3)$ fails the second inequality; $(4, 6)$ fails the second; $(0, 0)$ fails the first.
11. C — [Advanced Math: Nonlinear functions] A $6\%$ yearly increase multiplies by $1.06$ each year: $P(t) = 2400(1.06)^t$.
12. B — [PSDA: Two-variable data: models and scatterplots] The line $y = 2.5x + 10$ gives $2.5(14) + 10 = 45$. Choice C is the actual data point, not the prediction.
13. $-10$ — [Algebra: Linear functions] Slope $= \frac{5 - 11}{3 - 1} = -3$, so $f(x) = -3x + 14$ and $f(8) = -24 + 14 = -10$.
14. C — [Geometry/Trig: Lines, angles, and triangles] Scale factor $\frac{DE}{AB} = \frac{10}{6} = \frac{5}{3}$, so $EF = 9 \cdot \frac{5}{3} = 15$.
15. C — [Advanced Math: Systems of equations in two variables] $x + 3 = x^2 - 3x + 6$ gives $x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$, so $x = 1$ or $x = 3$; the points are $(1, 4)$ and $(3, 6)$.
16. A — [Algebra: Linear equations in two variables] Slope of $\ell$ is $\frac{7 - (-1)}{6 - 2} = 2$; a parallel line through $(0, 4)$ is $y = 2x + 4$.
17. 6 — [Advanced Math: Nonlinear functions] Vertex at $x = \frac{10}{2} = 5$; $f(5) = 25 - 50 + 31 = 6$.
18. A — [PSDA: One-variable data: distributions and measures of center and spread] Median is the average of the 10th and 11th values, both $4$. Mean $= \frac{6 + 15 + 24 + 20 + 24}{20} = 4.45 > 4$; the two values of $12$ pull the mean up.
19. B — [Geometry/Trig: Circles] Complete the square: $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 12 + 9 + 4 = 25$, so $r = 5$.
20. C — [Algebra: Systems of two linear equations] No solution means equal slopes: $\frac{3}{k} = \frac{6}{10}$, so $k = 5$ (and $\frac{7}{15} \ne \frac{1}{2}$, so the lines are distinct).
21. A — [Advanced Math: Equivalent expressions] $\frac{(x - 7)(x + 3)}{(x - 7)(x + 7)} = \frac{x + 3}{x + 7}$.
22. 9/17 — [PSDA: Probability and conditional probability] Of the $102$ students who prefer morning, $54$ are in grade 12: $\frac{54}{102} = \frac{9}{17} \approx .5294$ (either form is accepted).
23. D — [Geometry/Trig: Right triangles and trigonometry] $\sin A = \frac{5}{13}$ means the side opposite $A$ is $5$ and the hypotenuse is $13$, so the side opposite $B$ is $12$. Then $\tan B = \frac{12}{5}$.
24. B — [Advanced Math: Nonlinear equations in one variable] One solution when $k^2 - 4(4)(25) = 0$, so $k^2 = 400$ and $k = -20$ since $k < 0$.
25. 4 — [Advanced Math: Nonlinear functions] $g(x) = ab^x$: $\frac{g(3)}{g(1)} = b^2 = 9$, so $b = 3$ and $g(0) = \frac{g(1)}{3} = 4$.
26. A — [Algebra: Linear inequalities in one or two variables] Hours are limited ("at most" $\Rightarrow \le 40$) and earnings have a floor ("at least" $\Rightarrow \ge 1000$).
27. 5 — [Advanced Math: Nonlinear functions] $p + q = 6$ and $q - p = 4$ give $p = 1$, $q = 5$; $c = pq = 5$.
