Problem 4. A gym charges a \$25 sign-up fee plus \$15 per month.
| Months ($m$) | Total cost ($C$) |
|:------------:|:----------------:|
| 1            | \$40             |
| 3            |                  |
| 6            |                  |
(a) Complete the table.
(b) Write an equation for $C$ in terms of $m$.

Answer Key
1. The $y$-values are $-3, -1, 1, 3$.
[graph]
x: -5 to 5
y: -5 to 5
y = 2x - 1
points (-1, -3) (0, -1) (1, 1) (2, 3)
[/graph]
2. A — the boundary is solid because the inequality includes equality, and the shading is below the line.
3. (a) $f(0) = 4$ (the closed dot is on the right piece). (b) $0 \le y \le 4$
4. (a) \$70 and \$115 (b) $C = 25 + 15m$
